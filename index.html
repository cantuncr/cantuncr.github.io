01 — About

I'm a builder. I run CTSS LLC, a Wyoming-registered software & educational technology studio, and ship my own products: a pixel-grid ad marketplace, an AI legal network for lawyers, special-education software, a dental clinic OS, a white-label LMS and more. Before and alongside all that, I'm a QA engineer — so everything I ship is quality-first.

Quality is not a phase. It's a mindset.

I've tested products for global brands like Yum! Brands, led remote delivery teams across the US, EU and Türkiye, and designed a 6-month bootcamp that turns beginners into job-ready testers. Agile at heart, teacher by nature, founder by choice.
